# Code of conduct

HairPop welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and perspectives. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces is expected to help keep those spaces respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other people with respect and assume good faith.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about ideas and work.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and make room for others to participate.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing someone else's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness a concern, contact the [project maintainer](https://github.com/QuinntyneBrown) privately through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Include links or context that will help the maintainer understand what happened.

The maintainer will review reports, protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical, and may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation when needed. Reports will be considered in context and handled fairly.
