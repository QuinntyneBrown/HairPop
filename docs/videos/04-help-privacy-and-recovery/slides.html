Help, privacy and recovery

Privacy choices, themes, help, legal pages, error pages and keyboard support.

Where these screens live

Every page

Cookie banner, top bar theme toggle, footer links

Content

/help, /terms, /privacy

Errors

/404, /403, /500, /offline

First visit

  • Nothing optional stored before an answer
  • Nothing pre-selected
  • An answer is required to save
  • Toast with Change

Returning visitor

  • Footer Cookie preferences
  • Saved answer selected
  • Focus returns to the link

Theme

  • Follows the system until chosen
  • Pressed state on the toggle
  • Survives navigation and reload

Help

  • Four topics
  • Email support
  • ?ref=HP-1048 fills the subject

Terms and Privacy

  • No account needed
  • Effective date shown
  • Never invents policy text

Error pages

  • 404 → Explore styles
  • 403 → Sign in
  • 500 → reference and support
  • Offline → saved appointments

Keyboard

  • Skip to content on first Tab
  • Visible focus rings
  • Focus moves to the new heading
  • Page title announced

Things to remember

  • Optional data waits for an answer; change it from the footer
  • The theme follows the system, then remembers your choice
  • Help, terms and privacy never invent text
  • Error pages explain and offer a safe next step
  • Keyboard and screen readers are supported from the first Tab
