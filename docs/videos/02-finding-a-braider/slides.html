Matching braiders

  • Availability or “No times offered yet”
  • Rating read as one sentence
  • “Not yet rated” instead of a number
  • Area and service price

Search

  • Keeps the selected style
  • Term kept in the address
  • No matches offers Clear filters

Filter braiders

  • Maximum price and distance
  • Active filters shown as chips
  • Reopening shows what is applied
  • Cancel changes nothing

Validation and failure

  • Error summary links to the field
  • Lowest price for the style named
  • A failed save keeps every entry
  • Try again applies

No matches

  • Suggests a wider range
  • Clear filters restores the style

Braider profile

  • Identity, rating, area, travel
  • Services with prices
  • Before you book
  • Recent work and reviews
  • Studio address stays private

Reviews failed

  • One warning, profile stays
  • Retry reviews in place

New braider

  • Not yet rated
  • No portfolio photos yet
  • No reviews yet

Things to remember

  • Cards show availability, an honest rating, area and price
  • Search and filters keep the style; filters live in a dialog
  • The dialog validates, survives failures and keeps entries
  • Profiles keep the studio address private until a request is accepted
  • Missing reviews or photos get honest empty states
