Foundation

Shape

Corners grow with the element: 8px on controls, 12px on cards and dialogs, 16px on sheets, and fully round for pills, chips, badges and avatars.

  • Overview
  • Radii
  • Radius by element size
  • Border widths

Overview

HairPop is soft but not bubbly. The mocks used five radii and full rounding consistently; one stray value (a 2px top-only radius on the tab indicator) now uses --radius-xs, and circles drawn with 50% use --radius-full.

Nested corners: an element inside a padded container uses a smaller radius than its parent (photo md inside card lg), so the curves look concentric.

Usage

  • Choose the radius by the element's size and role from the table above; do not invent in-between values.
  • Joined controls (input groups) square the inner corners and keep the outer ones at --radius-md.
  • The focus ring is an outline, which follows the element's own radius.
  • Borders a person needs to see to operate a control are 1px --color-border-strong, at least 3:1 against the surface.
