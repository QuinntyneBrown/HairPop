Foundation

Motion

Motion in HairPop is quick and quiet: it confirms that something happened and shows where it went, then gets out of the way.

  • Overview
  • Durations
  • Easings
  • Choreography
  • Loops
  • Usage

Overview

Every transition reads a duration token and an easing token, so the whole product speeds up, slows down or stops in one place. Motion explains change: a dialog rises from where a phone thumb is, a toast slides up from the edge it lives on, a button presses down. It never decorates.

  • Durations are short. Colour and hover changes take 120ms, reveals 200ms, dialogs and sheets 320ms.
  • Easings are asymmetric. Things that enter decelerate (--ease-enter); things that leave accelerate (--ease-exit).
  • Loops (spinner, skeleton shimmer, live dot) have their own tokens because they repeat. Under reduced motion they pause, except the spinner.

  • Animate opacity, translate and scale; never width, height or top, which cause layout work. The progress bar's width is the single, documented exception.
  • One thing moves at a time. When a dialog opens, the page behind it does not move.
  • Entrances may be slower than exits; nobody waits for something to leave.

Loops

Loading indicators repeat, so they use loop tokens measured per cycle. They are not zeroed by reduced motion (a zeroed loop would flicker); decorative loops read animation-play-state: var(--motion-loop-state) instead.

Usage

  • Always write transition: <property> var(--duration-*) var(--ease-*). A raw duration or ease-in-out in component CSS is a bug.
  • Name the properties you transition; never transition: all.
  • Use the shared keyframes in components.css (spin, fade-in, fade-out, slide-up, scale-in, shimmer, indeterminate, pulse); do not add per-component copies.
  • A component never needs its own prefers-reduced-motion query: the tokens handle it.
  • No motion carries meaning on its own. A toast that slides in also says what happened; a pulsing dot also has a text label.

Accessibility

Reduced motion (WCAG 2.3.3)

When the operating system asks for reduced motion, tokens.css sets the fast, base, slow and deliberate durations to 0.01ms and --motion-loop-state to paused:

  • Transitions and entrances (dialogs, sheets, toasts, menus, card lifts) complete instantly; their end states are unchanged.
  • Skeleton shimmer, indeterminate progress and the live-dot pulse pause, so the skeleton still shows its shape and the progress bar still shows a segment.
  • The spinner keeps turning. It is the only sign that work is in progress, it is small, and it does not travel across the screen.
  • Smooth scrolling switches to auto.

Flashes and timing

Nothing flashes more than three times a second; the pulse dims to 35% once per 1.6s. Auto-dismissing toasts stay at least 8 seconds and pause on hover and focus (WCAG 2.2.1).
