Pattern

Tables and lists

Customers scan HairPop collections to choose: styles, braiders, appointments, updates. Lists of cards do most of that work; tables appear only when Maya compares the same facts across rows.

Overview

The mocks show four collections: the style gallery and matching braiders as card grids, my appointments as a list of row cards, and the notification center as a list of updates. Each sits under a filter row (search, chips, a Filters button) and, when long, ends with pagination that says how many there are ("Showing 1–3 of 126").

When to use which

CollectionUseWhy
Things chosen by look (styles, braiders)Story cards in .feature-gridThe photo is the information.
Maya’s own items with a status and one next stepRow cards (.card.appointment-row) or a listStatus badge, title, facts and action together.
Updates in time orderList with unread markersRead state and time matter more than layout.
Comparing the same 3+ facts across many rows (appointment history)TableColumns make comparison fast.
Facts about one thingDescription list or fact tilesKey and value, not rows.

  • Loading: skeletons in the collection’s own shape (card grid or rows); the filter row stays usable.
  • Empty: a first-run empty state replaces the collection; filters hide.
  • No results: filters stay visible with the applied values, and the empty state offers "Clear filters".
  • Long: pagination with a count. Prefer pages to infinite scroll so the footer and position are reachable and Back works.
  • Edge: long names wrap (never truncate a person’s name); missing photos fall back to the art panel at the same 3:2 ratio.

Responsive

  • Card grids: 1 column under 768px, 2 to 1023px, 3 from 1024px (.feature-grid).
  • Lists stay one column; a trailing badge moves to its own line rather than squeezing a title below half the row.
  • Row cards wrap the action under the text on phones.
  • Tables scroll horizontally inside .table-wrap; tables with four or fewer columns may switch to row cards under 768px when the page says so.
  • The pagination summary takes its own line under 640px.

Accessibility

  • Cards in a collection are <article> with a heading; each has one real link whose text names the item ("View Nia’s profile").
  • Filter chips are toggle buttons with aria-pressed, grouped and labelled; changing a filter announces the new count in a status region ("12 braiders").
  • Tables have a <caption>, scope on column headers, a row header (<th scope="row">) for the identifying cell, and aria-sort on the sorted column only; the sort control is a button. A table wider than its wrapper scrolls inside it, and the wrapper becomes a focusable region named by the caption.
  • Pagination is a labelled nav; the current page has aria-current="page".
  • Status is badge text plus colour: "Awaiting reply", "Completed", "Unread".

Content

  • Card summaries are one line of facts separated by " · ": rating, reviews, area, starting price.
  • Counts use numerals and en dashes: "Showing 1–3 of 126".
  • Statuses are short and specific: "Awaiting reply", "Confirmed", "Completed", "Cancelled".
  • Link text names the item: "View Nia’s profile", not "View profile" repeated three times.
