Pattern

Notifications

Booking news reaches Maya in two places: a toast while she is here, and the notification center so nothing is missed. Each update says what changed and links to it.

Overview

The mocks define four notification families: booking update (request received, time confirmed, time needs a change, update could not be sent), review feedback, account feedback and cookie consent, each in info, success, warning and danger tones, with an action, stacked, and persistent. Every booking update also lands in the notification center, reached from "Updates" in the top bar with its unread count.

Structure

Two live regions side by side: a polite one for news, an assertive one for failures. Newest toast at the bottom, at most three at once. The notification center keeps the record.

  • Stacked: up to three toasts; a fourth replaces the oldest non-danger toast. Toasts about the same request merge ("2 updates from Nia").
  • With action: one action, a verb; the toast stays while it has focus.
  • Leaving: fades and drops 8px over --duration-base; instant under reduced motion.
  • Unread / read in the center: unread has a dot and the accent marker; reading it updates the top bar count.

Responsive

  • Toasts: bottom right, 24rem, from 640px; full width along the bottom on phones, above env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
  • The cookie banner spans the bottom of the viewport at every width and the page adds bottom padding equal to its height so it never covers the footer.
  • The notification center list is single column at every width; actions wrap under the text on phones.

Accessibility

  • Polite news in a role="status" region; failures in a separate role="alert" region. Never nest one live region in another.
  • Auto-dismiss at no less than 8 seconds, paused on hover and focus; danger and persistent messages never auto-dismiss (2.2.1).
  • Toasts never take focus. Their action is reachable by Tab, and the same action exists in the notification center.
  • Toasts and banners never cover the focused element (2.4.11).
  • The unread count is part of the Updates link name ("Updates 2, 2 unread"); at zero the badge and hidden text are removed.

Content

  • Title: the change in two to four words ("Time confirmed"). Text: one sentence with the specifics ("Nia confirmed Sat 24 Oct at 10:00 am.").
  • Use Nia’s name; Maya chose her and recognises it.
  • Failures say what is safe first: "Your request is still saved."
  • Never call a request confirmed before Nia accepts it: "Request received", then "Time confirmed".
