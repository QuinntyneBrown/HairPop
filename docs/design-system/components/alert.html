Alert and banner

An alert explains a condition that affects part of the page, and stays until it no longer applies: you're offline, some details could not load, check the highlighted field.

WCAG 2.2 AAIcon + title + textStatus: stable

Overview

Use an alert when a message belongs to the page, or to one region of it, and must stay visible while it is true: "You're offline", "Some details are unavailable", "We couldn't save this change". Use a toast for passing feedback about an action, an inline message for one field, and an empty state when the whole region failed to load.

  • Info: context that helps ("Prices are estimates until Nia confirms").
  • Success: a lasting confirmation on the page ("Your changes are saved").
  • Warning: something limits what people can do ("You're offline. Connect before sending.").
  • Danger: something failed or must be fixed ("We couldn't save this change", the form error summary).
  • Banner: full-width, under the top bar, for conditions that affect the whole app (offline, maintenance).

Anatomy

  1. Icon20px, the tone's --color-*-icon; info, check-circle, warning or alert-circle.
  2. Title.alert__title, 16px semibold; the condition in a few words.
  3. TextOne or two sentences: what it means and what still works.
  4. Actions (optional)Up to two links or text buttons, inheriting the tone colour.
  5. Dismiss (optional)24px close button; only for alerts that may be ignored.
  6. ContainerTinted background, 1px tone border, 4px leading edge, --radius-md, 16px padding.

  • Persistent is the default: warning and danger alerts stay until the condition ends (back online, saved).
  • Dismissed alerts are removed from the DOM and remembered for the session; focus moves to the next focusable element in the region.

Responsive behaviour

  • Alerts fill their container at every width; icon and close stay top-aligned while the text wraps.
  • Actions wrap below the text on phones; at 360px two short actions still fit on one line.
  • Banners centre their content and wrap title and text onto separate lines below 640px; padding follows --layout-margin.

Theming

Component tokens --alert-bg, --alert-fg, --alert-border and --alert-icon map to the tone's --color-*-bg, -fg, -border and -icon. In dark mode the background becomes a deep tint (amber-950, red-950) with light text and a mid-tone edge.

/* A neutral note with the alert layout */
.alert--neutral { --alert-bg: var(--color-bg-subtle); --alert-fg: var(--color-fg-default); --alert-border: var(--color-border-default); --alert-icon: var(--color-fg-muted); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Alerts present when the page loads need no role; they are part of the content. An alert inserted after load uses role="status" (warning, info, success) or role="alert" (danger); see the APG Alert pattern. Status is icon + title + text, never colour alone.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabReaches links, actions and the dismiss button in order.
Enter / SpaceFollows a link or activates an action or dismiss.

Focus

The error summary takes focus on a failed submit (tabindex="-1", then focus()), and each item links to its field. It is a role="group" named by its title (aria-labelledby), not role="alert": the focus move already announces it, and a live role would announce it twice. Other alerts never take focus. Links inside use the shared ring.

Labelling

The title names the condition; the icon is aria-hidden. The dismiss button names the alert ("Dismiss: Some details are unavailable").

Motion

None. Alerts appear in place; they do not slide or pulse.

Touch

The dismiss button is 24px with the alert's padding around it; action links are at least 24px tall.

Content

  • Title: the condition, not a judgement: "You’re offline", "Some details are unavailable", "We couldn’t save this change".
  • Text: what it means and what is safe: "Your details remain here. Connect before sending."
  • Actions name the outcome: "Retry reviews", "Try again", never "Click here".
  • Error summaries say how many fields need attention and list each one in the field's words.
