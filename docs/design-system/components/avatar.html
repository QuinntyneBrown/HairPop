Avatar

An avatar puts a face, or the initials, beside a braider's or customer's name, so people recognise who they are booking with at a glance.

WCAG 2.2 AADecorative beside a nameStatus: stable

Overview

Use an avatar next to a person's name: Nia's profile header, a braider row in matching results, the reviewer on a review. It helps recognition; it never carries information on its own, so the name always sits beside it. Use a story card photo when the picture shows work (the braids themselves), not the person.

  • Image is the braider's own portrait. It is the default when a photo exists.
  • Initials ("NO" for Nia Okafor) are the fallback when there is no photo or it fails to load. Two letters, first and last name.
  • Icon (a person outline) stands in when the name is unknown, such as a signed-out account menu.
  • Group stacks up to three avatars with a "+N" overflow, for example braiders who offer a style.

Anatomy

  1. FrameCircle (--radius-full) sized by --avatar-size; the plum-50 fill shows while the image loads.
  2. ContentAn <img> cropped with object-fit: cover, or two initials, or a 24-unit icon.
  3. Status dot (optional)A quarter of the size, success colour, with a --border-width-thick (2px) ring in the surface colour. Never the only signal.

Variants

Choose by what you know about the person. A photo beats initials; initials beat an icon.

Sizes

Medium (40px) is the default in lists and rows. Small sits inline with body text and in reviewer groups; large leads a card; extra large is the profile header and grows from 48px to 64px at 768px.

  • Loading: the plum-50 frame shows while the image downloads and reserves the size, so nothing shifts. In a loading list use a circle skeleton instead.
  • Failed image: swap to initials on the image's error event; never show a broken-image icon.
  • Status: the dot means "available this week" on matching results and is always repeated in text ("Available Sat 24 Oct").

Responsive behaviour

  • Only .avatar--xl changes with the viewport: 48px on phones, 64px from --layout-breakpoint-md (768px), so the profile head keeps the name readable at 360px.
  • In a .profile-head the avatar and name wrap onto two lines when the name is long ("Amara Nkiruka Adeyemi-Williams"); the avatar never shrinks (flex: none).
  • Groups show at most three faces plus the overflow at every width. When the row is wider than its container (text at 200% on a phone), the group wraps onto a second row: each avatar overlaps the next with a negative inline-end margin and the group adds matching inline-end padding, so every row starts flush with the container.

Theming

Two component tokens drive the size: --avatar-size and --avatar-font. Initials read --color-fg-accent on --color-accent-subtle; the status dot reads --color-success-icon with a ring in --color-bg-surface. In dark mode the frame becomes plum-950 and the initials plum-300.

/* A neutral avatar for support staff */
.avatar--neutral { background: var(--color-bg-subtle); color: var(--color-fg-muted); }
/* A custom size in a reviewer list */
.review .avatar { --avatar-size: 2.25rem; }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A plain <span> with an <img> inside, or with initials. No ARIA pattern applies. A group that stands alone uses role="img" with one aria-label naming everyone.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the avatar; it is never focusable. When the avatar sits in a link, focus lands on the link.

Focus

None on the avatar. The surrounding link or card shows the shared ring.

Labelling

Beside a visible name the avatar is decorative: alt="" on the image, aria-hidden="true" on initials and icons, so a screen reader does not say "N O, Nia Okafor". Alone, the image's alt is the person's name ("Nia Okafor"), never "Portrait of a braider". The status dot is decorative because the status is also written out.

Contrast

Initials on avatar
Status dot on surface

Motion

None. Images do not fade in; the frame is visible from the first paint.

Forced colours

Backgrounds are replaced, so the avatar draws a CanvasText hairline border to keep its circle, and the status dot is drawn in CanvasText with forced-color-adjust: none.

Touch

Not a target. When an avatar is the only visible part of a link (an account menu), the link is at least 44px square.

Content

  • Initials are the first letters of the first and last name, uppercase: "NO", "AM", "JB". One letter for a single name.
  • Use the person's own photo; never stock imagery or a hairstyle photo in an avatar.
  • Alt text for a standalone avatar is the full name only. Do not describe the photo.
  • Overflow reads "+9", and the group label says "and 9 more braiders".
