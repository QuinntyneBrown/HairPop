Card

Cards group one subject on a raised surface: a style to explore, a braider to compare, an appointment to check. Every card answers one question and offers at most one main action.

Overview

Use a card when a piece of content stands on its own and could be moved, compared or repeated: a style in the gallery, a braider in the matching list, the appointment summary, a review. Do not use a card to frame a whole page or to fake a section heading; a page section is plain content on the canvas.

  • Default card holds a section of a detail page: "What to expect", "Before you book", "Prepare for your visit".
  • Story card leads with a 3:2 photo or art panel, then an eyebrow, a title, one line of facts and one link: the style gallery and matching braiders grids.
  • Interactive card makes the whole surface a target by stretching its one real link (.card__link); the card itself is never the control.
  • Row card (.appointment-row) puts the summary and its action on one line, wrapping on phones: My appointments.
  • Selected card marks the chosen option in a set, such as the time or braider picked in a flow.

Anatomy

  1. Media (optional)3:2 photo with real alt, or a decorative art panel (aria-hidden). --radius-md.
  2. Eyebrow (optional)Overline in --color-fg-accent: why this card is here ("Most explored", "Available Sat 24 Oct").
  3. TitleA heading at the right level for the page; .card__title (h4 role) in grids, the h3 role inside detail cards.
  4. SummaryOne line of facts in --color-fg-muted, separated by middle dots.
  5. ActionOne link or button that names its target ("Explore Knotless braids").
  6. Container--color-bg-surface, 1px --color-border-default, --radius-lg, --shadow-1, --space-6 padding.

Variants

Pick the variant by what the person does with the card: read it (default), choose from many (story or interactive), act on a record (row), or confirm a choice (selected).

Sizes

Cards have one padding (--space-6, 24px) and take their width from the grid they sit in. Use .card--flush for edge-to-edge media or a table, and .well (sunken, 16px padding) for a box inside a card. Never nest a card in a card.

States

Only interactive and selectable cards have states. Hover lifts the card 1px with --shadow-2 and a strong border; focus draws the shared ring around the whole card while its link has focus; selected uses the accent edge. Static cards have no hover and no disabled state.

In the dark theme the surface is grey 900 on a grey 950 canvas, so cards read as raised through lightness as well as their deeper shadow.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards fill their grid track. .feature-grid shows 1 column below 768px, 2 from 768px and 3 from 1024px; .detail-grid stacks the aside under the main card below 768px.
  • Padding stays 24px at every width; on a 360px phone with 16px page margins that leaves 278px of content, enough for every card in the mocks.
  • Row cards (.appointment-row) wrap: the action drops below the summary when the line is too narrow.
  • Media keeps its 3:2 ratio at every width, so the grid does not jump while images load.

Theming

The card reads four component tokens: --card-bg (--color-bg-surface), --card-border (--color-border-default), --card-shadow (--shadow-1) and --card-padding (--space-6). Hover and selected states change the component tokens, not the rules.

/* A quiet highlighted card, e.g. "Most explored" */
.card--highlight { --card-bg: var(--color-accent-subtle); --card-border: var(--color-accent-subtle-hover); --card-shadow: none; }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A card is an <article> when it can stand alone (a style, a braider, a review) or a <section aria-labelledby> when it is a named part of a page. It has no ARIA role of its own. An interactive card contains exactly one real link or button; the card is never role="button" or a wrapping <a> around block content with other controls inside.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the card's links and buttons; an interactive card is one tab stop (its .card__link).
EnterFollows the card's link.

Focus

When .card__link has focus the ring is drawn around the whole card (:has(.card__link:focus-visible)), so the target and the ring match.

Labelling

The card title is a real heading at the right level for the page (h2 in a grid under the page h1, h3 inside a section). Link text names the target ("Explore Knotless braids", not "Learn more"); a decorative arrow is aria-hidden. Photos have alt describing the hair, not "image". Selected state is said in text or aria-current, never only by the accent edge.

Contrast

Title on card
Summary on card
Eyebrow on card
Link on card
Selected edge on card
Focus ring on card

The default card border is decorative: the surface and shadow separate the card.

Motion

Hover lift, shadow and border transition over --duration-fast; reduced motion makes them instant.

Touch

Interactive cards are large targets by construction. In story cards the link is at least 24px tall and sits apart from other targets.

Content

  • Title: the thing, not a sentence ("Knotless braids", "Nia Okafor", "Medium knotless braids").
  • Summary: one line of facts in a fixed order (rating, reviews, area, price; or time, duration, price), separated by " · ".
  • Eyebrow: one short reason the card is here ("Most explored", "Available Sat 24 Oct"), sentence case in source, uppercase by style.
  • Prices are ranges with an en dash and no spaces ("$180–$240"); times read "Sat 24 Oct · 10:00 am".
  • One action per card, named by its target.
