Footer

The footer closes every HairPop screen with the brand line and the legal links people look for at the bottom: Terms and Privacy.

Overview

The footer sits at the end of .product-shell on every product page. It holds the brand line ("HairPop · Made for confident hair decisions") and secondary destinations that must always be findable but never compete with the task: Terms, Privacy and, as the product grows, Help and Cookie preferences.

  • Do not repeat the primary navigation here; the top bar owns it.
  • Do not put actions in the footer; a link that opens a dialog (Cookie preferences) is the only exception.

Anatomy

  1. Brand lineOne sentence in --text-body-sm, --color-fg-muted.
  2. Links.site-footer__links: a list of underlined links, 24px tall targets, 16px apart.
  3. ContainerTop hairline --color-border-default, padding --space-6 by --layout-margin; wraps with --space-3 gaps.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below about 560px the brand line and the links wrap onto two lines, both left-aligned; the links themselves wrap as a row with 4px row gaps.
  • Wider, the brand line sits left and the links right on one line (justify-content: space-between).
  • Side padding follows --layout-margin so the footer lines up with the page content and the top bar.
  • On pages with a fixed bottom element (toasts, cookie banner), the footer keeps its own space; fixed elements add env(safe-area-inset-bottom) and never cover footer links when focused.

Theming

The footer reads --color-fg-muted (text and links), --color-fg-default (link hover) and --color-border-default (top rule). It sits on the canvas, not a surface, so in the dark theme it is grey 350 text on the grey 950 canvas with a grey 750 rule.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<footer class="site-footer"> as a direct child of the shell (outside <main>) is the page's contentinfo landmark. Links sit in a <ul> so screen readers announce "list, 2 items". No ARIA needed.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabReaches the footer links last, in visual order.
EnterFollows the link (or opens the cookie preferences dialog).

Focus

The shared 2px ring with 2px offset. Fixed toasts and banners must not obscure a focused footer link (WCAG 2.4.11).

Labelling

Link text is the destination's name ("Privacy", "Terms"), matching the page's <h1> ("Privacy at HairPop", "Terms of use"). The decorative "·" separators of the mocks are not needed once links are a list.

Contrast

Footer text and links on page
Hovered link
Focus ring on page

Motion

None; colour changes are instant.

Touch

Links are at least 24px tall (--target-min) with 16px between them horizontally and 4px between wrapped rows; the wrapped rows remain 24px apart edge to edge because each target is 24px tall.

Content

  • The brand line stays "HairPop · Made for confident hair decisions" everywhere.
  • Link labels are one or two words in sentence case: "Terms", "Privacy", "Cookie preferences", "Help".
  • Order: Help, Cookie preferences, Terms, Privacy. Never more than six links.
