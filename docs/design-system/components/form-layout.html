Form layout

Form layout arranges fields, the error summary and actions into a readable form panel: one column, labels on top, one primary action, and a summary that links straight to each problem.

Overview

Every HairPop form (sign in, booking request, care and review, account settings) uses the same frame: a .form-panel card capped at --layout-form-max (38rem), a heading, an optional error summary, a single column of form fields 16px apart, and a .form-actions bar with one primary action. Filter rows above results are the one inline form.

  • Use a page form for multi-field tasks; use a dialog form only for one or two fields tied to the page behind it ("Request a change").
  • Validate with novalidate and the system's messages, not the browser's bubbles, so copy and timing are consistent.

Anatomy

  1. Panel and heading.card.form-panel, 38rem max, centred; heading in the h3 role.
  2. FieldsOne column in a .stack, 16px apart.
  3. ContextEstimates or consequences, muted, just above the actions.
  4. Actions bar.form-actions: primary full width on phones, end-aligned from 768px.

Forms take one width (38rem, the form maximum) and the medium control size; dense forms are not part of HairPop's customer journey.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px: one column, the panel spans the content width, the primary action is full width and first; secondary actions follow below it.
  • From 768px: .form-row may pair two short fields; actions align to the end with the primary last.
  • The panel never exceeds 38rem, so line length and eye travel stay short at every width.

Theming

The panel is a card (--card-bg, --card-padding); the summary is a danger alert with a 4px leading edge (--border-width-accent). Form layout adds no colours of its own.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<form novalidate> labelled by its panel heading; filter rows use role="search". The error summary is a focusable region (tabindex="-1") labelled by its title; it is focused by script after submit, not with autofocus.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through fields in visual order, then the actions.
EnterIn a single-line field, submits the form.
Enter on a summary linkMoves focus to that field.

Focus

After a failed submit focus lands on the summary; after success it lands on the confirmation heading. Focus is never sent back to the top of the page.

Labelling

The summary title counts the problems ("Check 2 answers"); each link repeats its field's error. Submitting progress is announced in a role="status" region; the button keeps its label (SC 4.1.3).

Motion

The summary appears without animation; the page does not scroll-animate to it (focus moves it into view).

Touch

The full-width primary action on phones is 40px tall or more; summary links are 24px targets.

Content

  • Panel headings name the content ("Your details"), not the action.
  • Put consequences next to the action: "Estimate: $180–$240 · about 5 hours. Nia confirms the final price and time."
  • Summary title: "Check 1 answer" / "Check 2 answers"; each item says what to do.
  • Success says what happened and what happens next, and never calls a pending request an appointment.
