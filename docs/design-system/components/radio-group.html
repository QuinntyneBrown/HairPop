Radio group

A radio group asks for exactly one answer from a short list that people should compare: a 1-to-5 rating, one of Nia's offered times, a braid size.

WCAG 2.2 AANative <input type="radio">Status: stable

Overview

Use a radio group when exactly one option must be chosen and there are two to five options worth seeing together: rating a visit, choosing small, medium or large braids, picking one of Nia’s offered times. Use a select for longer lists and checkboxes when several can be chosen.

  • Vertical is the default and handles long labels.
  • Horizontal suits short, ordered labels such as ratings 1 to 5.
  • Card carries a title and a description, such as a time with its estimate.

Anatomy

  1. LegendThe question; label font in a <fieldset>.
  2. Radio20px circle; checked fills with the accent and shows a centred dot.
  3. Option labelBody text; the whole row is clickable.

Sizes

Radios have one size: a 20px control in a row at least 24px tall; the card variant adds padding, not size.

Both themes

The theme previews force the checked look with data-state="checked" because the same markup appears twice and one radio group cannot hold two checked options.

Responsive behaviour

  • Horizontal groups wrap; if options wrap onto three lines at 360px, switch to vertical.
  • Card options are full width on phones and may sit two per row from 768px.

Theming

Shares the checkbox's tokens: a --color-border-strong ring, --color-accent fill and --color-fg-on-accent dot. The card variant adds --color-accent-subtle when checked.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native radios sharing a name, in a <fieldset> with <legend>; see the APG Radio Group pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves into the group (to the checked radio, or the first) and out of it.
↓ / →Checks the next option, wrapping.
↑ / ←Checks the previous option, wrapping.
SpaceChecks the focused option if none is checked.

Focus

The ring surrounds the circle; on cards it stays on the radio so it is clear which control moves.

Labelling

The legend asks the question; option labels answer it. Ratings label their top end ("5 (excellent)") so the numbers have meaning.

Contrast

Ring on card
Checked fill
Option text on checked card

Motion

The fill transitions over --duration-fast.

Touch

Each label row is at least 24px tall; rating options sit 24px apart; cards are 52px or taller.

Content

  • Legends are questions or nouns: "Rating, 1 to 5", "Preferred time".
  • Options are parallel and mutually exclusive; times read "Sat 24 Oct · 10:00 am".
  • Pre-select an option only when there is a safe, likely default (the time Maya first asked for).
