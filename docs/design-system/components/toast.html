Toast

A toast confirms that something happened, or failed, without taking people away from what they were doing: Request received, Time confirmed, Review not posted.

WCAG 2.2 AArole="status" / role="alert" regionsStatus: stable

Overview

Use a toast for brief feedback about an action the person just took, or an update they are waiting for: "Request received", "Time confirmed", "Details updated". Use an inline alert when the message belongs to one part of the page or must stay (offline, partial data), a dialog when a decision is required, and the notification centre for anything people need to find later.

  • Info: news with no judgement ("Request received", "Review ready").
  • Success: the action worked ("Time confirmed", "Review posted"). Dismisses after 8 seconds.
  • Warning: something needs a look but nothing failed ("Time needs a change"). Has an action.
  • Danger: the action failed ("Update could not be sent"). Never auto-dismisses; says the work is safe and offers Retry.

Anatomy

  1. Status icon20px, the tone's --color-*-icon-inverse; tone is never colour alone.
  2. TitleLabel font; what happened, two to four words.
  3. TextBody small; the specific detail (who, when, what next).
  4. Action (optional)One underlined text button: Retry, Undo, View appointment.
  5. Dismiss24px close button, always present, labelled with the title.
  6. Timer bar (auto-dismiss only)2px, 40% of the text colour; pauses on hover and focus.

Variants

Booking updates from the mocks in each tone. Toasts are inverse surfaces (dark on a light page, light on a dark page) so they read above any content.

Sizes

Toasts have one size: 24rem wide from 640px, full width minus 16px margins below it.

States

A toast slides up on entry (--duration-base, enter easing), waits, and fades down on exit. Hover and focus inside it pause the timer. The action and dismiss buttons have the usual button states; focus is forced here with data-state.

  • Auto-dismiss: info and success toasts without an action leave after 8 seconds (WCAG 2.2.1), pausing while hovered or focused. Toasts with an action stay at least 10 seconds.
  • Persistent: danger toasts, and anything that asks for a choice (cookie consent), stay until dismissed or acted on. They show no timer bar.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below --layout-breakpoint-sm (640px) the region spans the bottom of the screen with 16px margins and respects env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
  • From 640px the region docks bottom right, 24rem wide, 24px from the edges.
  • .toast-region--top moves the region to the top, for screens with a sticky bottom action bar.
  • Toasts never cover the focused element or a primary action (WCAG 2.4.11).

Theming

Component tokens --toast-bg (--color-bg-inverse), --toast-fg (--color-fg-inverse) and --toast-icon (the tone's --color-*-icon-inverse). In light mode the toast is grey-900 with 300-step icons; in dark mode it flips to grey-100 with 600/700-step icons, so it still stands out from a dark page.

/* A raised (non-inverse) toast for a calm surface */
.toast--raised { --toast-bg: var(--color-bg-surface-raised); --toast-fg: var(--color-fg-default); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Two live regions exist on every page from load: <div class="toast-region" role="status" aria-live="polite"> for info, success and warning, and a second region with role="alert" for danger. Toasts are inserted into the right region; a toast never carries its own live role inside another live region (APG Alert).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabReaches the action and dismiss buttons in DOM order; focus inside pauses the timer.
Enter / SpaceActivates the action or dismisses.
EscDismisses the focused toast and returns focus to where it was before.

Focus

Toasts never take focus when they appear. After a dismiss, focus returns to the element that was focused before the person tabbed into the toast, or to the next toast.

Labelling

The title and text are announced together. The dismiss button names the toast ("Dismiss: Time confirmed"); the action names its result ("View appointment", "Retry"), not "View update".

Motion

Slide-up entry and fade-down exit over --duration-base; reduced motion zeroes both, so toasts appear and disappear in place.

Touch

The dismiss and action buttons are 24px targets with at least 12px between them. Swipe to dismiss is an enhancement; the close button is always there.

Content

  • Title: what happened, two to four words: "Time confirmed", "Review posted", "Changes not saved".
  • Text: the specific detail in one sentence: who, when, what next. "Nia confirmed Sat 24 Oct at 10:00 am."
  • Failures say the work is safe and what to do: "Your words are saved. Please try again." plus Retry.
  • One action at most; name its result. Offer Undo only when the action can be undone.
  • No "Success!" or "Error!" titles; the icon and tone already say that.

Drift fixed during extraction: toasts had no status icon, so the tone was colour only on the dark toast (system adds the tone icon and keeps a title); the role="status" region wrapped a danger toast with its own role="alert" (nested live regions; system uses separate polite and assertive regions); success toasts offered "Undo" for actions the customer cannot undo, such as Nia confirming a time; with-action toasts used the generic "View update"; the close button was a "×" glyph labelled "Dismiss notification" (system: close icon labelled with the toast title); toast text was dimmed to 90% opacity (removed; contrast comes from tokens).
