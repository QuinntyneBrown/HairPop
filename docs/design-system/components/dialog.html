Dialog

A dialog interrupts the page to finish one short task or confirm one decision: filter braiders, review a request, cancel an appointment. Everything behind it waits.

WCAG 2.2 AAAPG Dialog (modal)Angular CDK DialogStatus: stable

Overview

Use a dialog when the person must finish or decide something before going on, and the task fits on one short screen: two or three fields, or one decision with consequences. Use a page for anything longer (booking a request is a page, not a dialog), a toast to report an outcome, and an inline alert for information that needs no decision. In the product, dialogs open through the Angular CDK Dialog; never hand-roll a modal.

  • Form dialog collects a few values and applies them: "Filter braiders", "Request a change", "Report a review", "Cookie preferences".
  • Confirmation dialog asks the person to check details before an important but safe action: "Review request" before Nia receives it.
  • Destructive dialog confirms a loss with a danger icon and a danger button that names the loss: "Cancel request", "Sign out".

Anatomy

  1. Backdrop--color-bg-backdrop scrim at --z-overlay; the page behind is inert.
  2. Header: title and description.dialog__title (h3 role) is the accessible name; the sentence under it is the description.
  3. Close buttonGhost icon button, aria-label="Close dialog"; same as Esc.
  4. BodyFields, alerts or a short paragraph; --space-4 × --space-6 padding; scrolls when tall.
  5. FooterSafe action then the confirm; sticky at the bottom; top divider.

Sizes

Width caps only; the panel is always full width on phones. Pick the smallest width that keeps fields on one line.

SizeClassMax widthUse for
Small.dialog__panel--sm24rem (384px)Destructive and yes/no confirmations: Cancel request, Sign out.
Medium (default).dialog__panel32rem (512px)Form dialogs with one to three fields: Filter braiders, Request a change.
Large.dialog__panel--lg48rem (768px)Reading-heavy content such as cookie categories with descriptions.

States

The dialog's own states live in its footer and body: the confirm button carries busy, the body carries invalid and failed messages. Hover, focus and active belong to the controls inside (see button and text field).

StateWhat changesFooter
DefaultInitial focus on the first field, or on the safe action in a destructive dialog.
HoverOnly the hovered control changes.
FocusThe shared ring on the focused control; focus is trapped inside the panel.
BusyConfirm sets aria-busy="true" and keeps its label; Esc, close and backdrop are ignored until the request settles.
InvalidAn error summary at the top of the body takes focus; each field gets aria-invalid and its message.
FailedA danger alert at the top of the body says the change was not made and entries are kept; the confirm becomes "Try again".
Disabled confirmAvoid. Keep the confirm enabled and explain on activation.

The dark panel is --color-bg-surface-raised (grey 800), lighter than the grey 900 surface it covers, so it reads as raised even under a darker scrim.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below --layout-breakpoint-sm (640px) the panel becomes a bottom sheet: full width, --radius-xl top corners, slides up, and pads the bottom by env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
  • From 640px it is centred with 24px around it and --radius-lg corners; it scales in.
  • The panel never exceeds the viewport height minus 48px; the body scrolls and the footer stays pinned (position: sticky).
  • Footer buttons share the row; on phones they stretch and wrap with the confirm first in reading order (wrap-reverse).

Theming

The panel reads --dialog-bg (--color-bg-surface-raised) and --dialog-width; the backdrop reads --color-bg-backdrop, darker and more opaque in the dark theme. Icon chips use status tokens (--color-danger-bg/--color-danger-icon, --color-info-bg/--color-info-icon).

/* A wider filter dialog without a new class */

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Dialog (Modal): role="dialog" (or role="alertdialog" for destructive confirmations), aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby the title and aria-describedby the description. Everything behind is inert. The Angular CDK Dialog provides the focus trap, Esc handling and focus restoration. (The live examples on this page omit aria-modal because they are not modal here.)

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the dialog's controls and wraps; focus never leaves the panel.
EscCloses the dialog like the close button, unless it is busy.
EnterSubmits a form dialog from a text field; never confirms a destructive dialog by default.

Focus

On open, focus moves to the first field in a form dialog, or to the safe action ("Keep request") in a destructive one. On invalid submit, focus moves to the error summary. On close, focus returns to the control that opened the dialog.

Labelling

The title names the task ("Filter braiders", "Cancel request HP-1048?"). The description says what happens. The icon chip is decorative (its SVG is aria-hidden). The close button is named "Close dialog".

Motion

The backdrop fades in over --duration-base; the panel scales in (or slides up as a sheet) over --duration-slow with --ease-enter. Reduced motion makes both instant.

Touch

The close button is 40px square; footer buttons stretch on phones and are 40px tall. Tapping the backdrop closes a form dialog but not a busy or destructive one.

Content

  • Title: the task as a noun phrase ("Filter braiders") or the decision as a question ("Cancel request HP-1048?").
  • Description: one sentence on the consequence, naming people and times ("Nia will see that you no longer need Sat 24 Oct at 10:00 am.").
  • Confirm: a verb phrase naming the result ("Show braiders", "Send change request", "Cancel request"), never "OK" or "Confirm".
  • Safe action: what staying means ("Keep request", "Stay signed in"); "Cancel" is ambiguous next to "Cancel request".
  • Failure: say what did not happen and that entries are kept ("We couldn’t cancel this request. Your request is unchanged.").
