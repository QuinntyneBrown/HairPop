Switch

A switch turns a setting on or off immediately, with no save button: email updates about requests, a reminder the day before an appointment.

WCAG 2.2 AArole="switch" on a checkboxStatus: core set

Overview

Use a switch for a setting that takes effect the moment it changes, like notification preferences in account settings. If the change only applies after "Save changes", use a checkbox instead; the cookie-preferences dialog, which saves on "Save preferences", correctly uses a checkbox. Its home is the notification preferences card in account settings.

Anatomy

  1. Track44 × 24px pill; --color-border-strong off, --color-accent on.
  2. Thumb20px circle in the surface colour with --shadow-1; slides 20px.
  3. LabelNames the setting, not the action.

Variants

Label right (inline), and label left in a settings row (.switch--end) with a description.
HTML
<label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch" checked><span>Email me when Nia replies</span></label>
<div class="card" style="width: 100%;"><label class="switch switch--end"><input type="checkbox" role="switch"><span class="choice__text"><span>Appointment reminders</span><small class="text-muted">A text the day before Sat 24 Oct at 10:00 am.</small></span></label></div>

Sizes

Switches have one size: a 44 × 24px track in a row at least 24px tall.

States

ValueDefaultHoverFocusDisabled
Off
On

A switch has no invalid state: it cannot be wrong. If saving the change fails, flip it back and say so in a toast ("Reminders not updated. Try again.").

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

  • In settings lists the switch sits at the end of a full-width row (.switch--end), so labels align on the left. The label keeps at least 10em beside the switch; when it cannot (a narrow card, 200% text) the switch wraps beneath the label at the start instead of pushing the label off the card. The DOM order stays input, then text.
  • Descriptions wrap under the label; the switch stays vertically centred while it shares the row.

Theming

Component tokens: --switch-track (--color-border-strong), --switch-track-on (--color-accent), --switch-thumb-bg (--color-bg-surface), and the sizes --switch-width, --switch-height and --switch-thumb. In dark the thumb is grey 900 on a plum 300 track.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<input type="checkbox" role="switch"> inside its label: screen readers announce "switch, on/off". See the APG Switch pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFocuses the switch.
SpaceToggles it; the change applies immediately.

Focus

The shared ring follows the track's pill shape.

Labelling

The label names the setting ("Appointment reminders"), never the action ("Turn on reminders"), so "on" and "off" read correctly. Confirm the saved change in a polite live region.

Contrast

Off track on card
Thumb on off track
Thumb on on track

Forced colours

System colours would erase the track and thumb backgrounds, so the switch sets its own: a CanvasText hairline edge on the track and a CanvasText thumb; on, the track is Highlight with a HighlightText thumb (forced-color-adjust: none). The focus ring maps to Highlight.

Motion

The thumb slides and the track recolours over --duration-base (200ms); reduced motion makes both instant. Position, not just colour, shows the state.

Touch

The whole label row toggles the switch; the track alone is 44px wide.

Content

  • Labels are nouns for settings: "Email updates", "Appointment reminders".
  • Descriptions say what "on" means with real detail: "A text the day before Sat 24 Oct at 10:00 am."
  • Do not add "On" / "Off" text beside the switch; the role announces it.

Sources

Drift fixed during extraction: the kit's switch had no hover state and no role="switch" in its markup; the thumb travel was a raw 1.25rem (now computed from the component tokens); the row-reverse settings row pushed a long label off the card at 200% text (now it wraps); the on switch vanished in forced colours (now it has its own forced-colours rules).
