Spinner

A spinner says “working on it” for a short wait that has no shape yet: sending a message to Nia, checking an email address, refreshing a list in place.

WCAG 2.2 AArole="status"Status: stableCore set

Overview

Use a spinner for waits of roughly one to ten seconds where nothing better can stand in: a small region refreshing in place ("Checking Nia’s availability…"), an action inside a row, or the button that was pressed (the button draws its own spinner through aria-busy; see Button).

  • Loading a page or a list whose layout you know? Use a skeleton; it shows the shape and avoids layout shift.
  • A long task you can measure (uploads)? Use a progress bar.
  • Never replace a whole page with a centred spinner.

Anatomy

  1. ArcQuarter of the ring in --color-accent; turns once per --duration-loop-spin.
  2. TrackThe rest of the ring in --color-border-default, 2px (3px at large).
  3. Status textVisible or visually hidden; the element with role="status" announces it.

Variants

The spinner is decorative (aria-hidden); the text beside it, visible or hidden, carries the meaning.

Loading reviews Sending your message to Nia…
Standalone with visually hidden text, inline with visible text, and the button's own loading state.
HTML
<span role="status"><span class="spinner" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="visually-hidden">Loading reviews</span></span>
<span class="spinner-inline" role="status"><span class="spinner spinner--sm" aria-hidden="true"></span>Sending your message to Nia…</span>
<button class="btn btn--primary" type="button" aria-busy="true">Send request</button>

Sizes

Match the spinner to the text or region it stands for: small beside body-small text, medium beside body text and in rows, large in the middle of a card or empty region.

States

The spinner is not interactive (no hover, focus, active or disabled). It is either present while work runs or removed when it ends, replaced by the result or a message.

LoadingLoadingLoadingSending…
  • Show the spinner only after about 300ms so fast responses do not flash it, and keep it at least 500ms once shown.
  • When the wait passes ten seconds, add text that says what is still happening, or switch to a progress bar.

Responsive behaviour

  • Sizes do not change with the viewport; the spinner follows the text size it sits beside.
  • Inline status text wraps under the spinner's row at 360px rather than truncating; keep it under about 40 characters.
  • A large spinner centred in a card keeps the card's loaded height (set by its skeleton or minimum height), so nothing jumps when content arrives.

Theming

The arc reads --color-accent and the track --color-border-default; the size is the component token --spinner-size. In the dark theme the arc becomes plum 300 on a grey 750 track. Inside a button the spinner is drawn by the button in --btn-fg, so it always matches the label colour.

/* A spinner on an inverse toast */
.toast .spinner { border-color: var(--color-border-inverse); border-top-color: var(--color-fg-inverse); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Wrap the spinner in an element with role="status" (a polite live region, WAI-ARIA status) that holds the text; the ring itself is aria-hidden="true". Mark the region being refreshed with aria-busy="true" until it is done. The live region must exist before its text changes, or the change is not announced.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabNot focusable. Focus stays on the control that started the work.

Focus

Never move focus to a spinner. When the result arrives, leave focus where it was unless the result is a new view, in which case move focus to its heading.

Labelling

Say what is loading: "Loading reviews", "Sending your message to Nia". Generic "Loading" is acceptable only when the surrounding region's heading already says what. Announce the end too ("Reviews loaded" or the error).

Contrast

Arc on surface
Arc on page
Inline status text on surface

Motion

One revolution per --duration-loop-spin (800ms), linear. Under prefers-reduced-motion the spinner keeps turning: it is the only sign work is in progress and it does not travel across the screen. Shimmer and pulse loops, by contrast, pause.

Touch

Not a target. Never put a spinner in place of a button people still need to press.

Content

  • Use a present-participle phrase that names the thing: "Checking Nia’s availability…", "Sending your request…".
  • End with an ellipsis character (…), not three dots.
  • Replace the text with the outcome when the wait ends: "Saved", "Couldn’t refresh. Try again."
  • Do not add humour or apologies to waits under ten seconds.

Sources

No mock shows a standalone spinner; it is part of the core set. The only spinner in the mocks is the button's busy state:

  • pages/booking-request/submitting (busy submit button)
  • dialogs/cancel-appointment/busy (busy danger button in a dialog)

Drift fixed during extraction: the spin duration was a raw 0.8s (now --duration-loop-spin); the mocks' global reduced-motion override cut every animation to one 0.01ms iteration, which froze the spinner mid-turn (spinners now keep turning); the button spinner was 1.1em (now 1.25em, the icon size); busy buttons replaced their label with "Working…" (labels now stay).
