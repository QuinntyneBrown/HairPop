Sizes

One height (56 px, --layout-topbar-height) at every breakpoint; only the contents change. The bar grows only when enlarged text wraps it (D-8).

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state (and, for the current link and brand hover, the hover colour written inline); real pages use :hover, :focus-visible, aria-current and aria-expanded. The menu button is shown here without topbar__menu so it stays visible at every width.

Responsive

The phone menu panel is a CDK Overlay pane attached under the bar (D-3). Here it is drawn with a specimen-only absolutely positioned wrapper; the panel covers the page content instead of pushing it down. In the scrolled frame the bar is sticky and the panel stays attached to it.

AC-18, AC-20 and AC-27 are behaviours (outside pointer, widening, reduced motion); the frames show the resting results.
