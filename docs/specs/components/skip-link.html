In place: revealed by the first Tab, 16 px from the top-left corner, over the sticky top bar at --z-tooltip. Enter moves focus to <main id="main"> without changing the URL; Tab instead hides it and moves to the brand; a click does what Enter does. AC-3, AC-4, AC-5, AC-6, AC-7

Sizes

One size: the label in --text-label with --space-2 by --space-4 padding, measured 134 × 39 px for “Skip to content”.

States

Shown with .skip-link--demo and forced with data-state. Hover and active happen only after focus has revealed it; the label takes the link hover colour and stays un-underlined. A skip link whose target is missing keeps focus and logs a development error. Behind a modal dialog it is inert with the page and never receives focus. Screen readers list it first among the page’s links.
