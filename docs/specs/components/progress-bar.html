Progress bar

How far a task of known length has come, or that a long task is still moving, ending in success or danger with the label saying the outcome. Requirements: progress-bar.md. Design system: progress-bar.

Sizes

One size: an 8 px track (--space-2), always the container's full width.

States

The bar is not interactive. Its states are where the task is; the action's states are Button's.

Responsive

The bar has no breakpoint rules; the label row is what changes. The “required” frames apply D-4 (the row wraps); the “current (drift)” frames show today's stylesheet squeezing the count at 320 px and overflowing the card at 360 px with text at 200 %.
