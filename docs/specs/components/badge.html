Badge

A read-only status in a word or two (“Awaiting reply”, “Confirmed”), the unread count on “Updates”, and the rare solid “New”. Requirements: badge.md. Design system: badge.

The appointment lifecycle, identical on every screen. “Declined” is danger (D-4); “Completed” is neutral (D-3). AC-4, AC-5, AC-6, AC-7, AC-8

Solid and count

NewUpdates 2, 2 unreadUpdates 99+, 120 unreadUpdates
Solid “New”; the count form shows the number only, followed by the link’s hidden “, 2 unread” (D-5): 2, 120 shown as “99+” with hidden “, 120 unread”, and 0 rendered hidden (the last link has no visible badge). AC-10, AC-11, AC-12, AC-13, AC-14

Sizes

One size: 24 px minimum height, 12 px overline label. A single-digit count is a 24 px circle.

States and contexts

A badge is static and never focusable. Container states are forced with data-state; the zero count uses the real hidden attribute.
