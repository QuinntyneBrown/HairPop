Radio group

Exactly one answer from a short list: a 1-to-5 rating, one of Nia’s offered times, a braid size, on native radios. Requirements: radio-group.md. Design system: radio-group.

Variants

Rating, 1 to 5
Horizontal rating inside the form field’s fieldset: five native radios sharing one name, values 1–5; the top end is labelled (D-14). AC-1, AC-5, AC-6
Preferred time
Nia’s offered times as cards, --space-2 apart (D-6), one per row at every width (D-7). The name is the time; the estimate is the description. AC-3, AC-4, AC-19
Braid size
Vertical (default) group. AC-2

Sizes

Radios have one size: a 20 px control in a row at least 24 px tall; the card variant adds padding, not size.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state; the rest use the real attributes (checked, disabled, aria-invalid).

Preferred time
Keyboard: Tab lands on the checked option; the arrow keys move and check, wrapping from “5 (excellent)” to “1”; Space checks when none is. The ring stays on the radio, also on cards. AC-14, AC-15, AC-16, AC-17, AC-18

Both themes

AC-20, AC-21 (forced colours: checked Highlight, rings CanvasText), AC-25 (reduced motion: instant)

Responsive

Every frame is a real viewport. Horizontal groups wrap at every width (D-8); cards stay one per row (D-7). Cards sit --space-2 apart (D-6), and at 200 % text a card’s time and estimate move under the radio (D-15).
