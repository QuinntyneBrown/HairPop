# L1 — High-Level Requirements

## Scope and source

These requirements cover the HairPop customer platform represented by [`docs/mocks/README.md`](../mocks/README.md) and [`docs/mocks/manifest.json`](../mocks/manifest.json), plus the minimum braider capabilities described in [`docs/7-star-redesign.md`](../7-star-redesign.md) needed to complete the customer journey. The mocks define the customer-facing screens, states, dialogs, notifications, and visual direction. Their sample names, dates, prices, availability, addresses, and legal copy are illustrative rather than product policy or live data. A new braider studio, admin product, payments, and the redesign's explicitly deferred capabilities are outside this scope.

## L1-001: Discover hairstyles

Customers must be able to browse and search a curated hairstyle gallery, narrow it by style category, and inspect each style's variations, expected duration, typical price, upkeep, and factors that affect a final quote.

## L1-002: Find braiders for a style

Customers must be able to see braiders who explicitly offer a selected style, narrow results by relevant location and price criteria, and understand the service and availability information behind each result.

## L1-003: Evaluate a braider

Customers must be able to inspect a braider's offered services, relevant work, reviews, service area, preparation guidance, policies, and route to requesting an appointment. Customers must be able to report a review for moderation.

## L1-004: Manage customer identity

Customers must be able to create an account, sign in and out, verify an email address, recover access, and maintain the contact details needed for appointments.

## L1-005: Request an appointment

Authenticated customers must be able to select a braider's offered service and time, provide essential details, review the estimate and applicable policy, and submit one trackable request. The platform must distinguish a pending request from an accepted appointment.

## L1-006: Manage appointments

Customers must be able to see upcoming and past appointments, inspect their current status and agreed details, request a change, cancel where permitted, and repeat a service. Braiders must be able to accept or decline requests and respond to changes so customer-facing status remains accurate.

## L1-007: Follow up after a visit

Customers must be able to access care and preparation guidance, rate and review a completed appointment, and see the outcome of their submission.

## L1-008: Keep braider offerings current

Braiders must be able to maintain the services, indicative prices and durations, portfolio material, service area, policies, and offered availability used by discovery and booking.

## L1-009: Communicate changes and feedback

The platform must provide customers with relevant appointment, review, account, and privacy updates through a notification center and timely on-screen feedback, with clear read and unread states.

## L1-010: Provide help, legal information, and privacy choices

Customers must be able to find help for requests, pricing, changes, and reviews; access current terms and privacy information; and set or revise optional cookie and usage preferences.

## L1-011: Handle incomplete and unavailable experiences

Every customer-facing journey must present useful loading, empty, no-results, partial-data, validation, submission, failure, offline, and success states where applicable, and provide a safe next action. Unavailable pages and unauthorized resources must have clear destination pages.

## L1-012: Protect accounts and customer data

The platform must authenticate protected actions, authorize access to user-owned information, validate untrusted input, protect private data, and resist common abuse without hiding intentionally public discovery content.

## L1-013: Provide an accessible, responsive experience

The customer platform must support keyboard and assistive-technology use, light and dark themes, and usable layouts from extra-small mobile through extra-large desktop viewports, following the mock design's semantics and content hierarchy.

## L1-014: Operate reliably and efficiently

The platform must keep discovery and account workflows responsive, prevent duplicate or ambiguous state-changing actions, make operational failures diagnosable, and recover cleanly from interrupted requests and temporary service outages.
