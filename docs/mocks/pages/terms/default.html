How HairPop helps customers and independent braiders connect.

Using HairPop

HairPop helps customers discover independent braiders and send appointment requests. A request is pending until the braider accepts it.

Prices shown before confirmation are estimates. The braider confirms appointment details and policies directly with you.

Requests and reviews

You can update contact details and review your appointments in your account. Contact support@hairpop.example for help with your information or an appointment.
